I am a medical student interested in embodied intelligence, vision-language-action models, test-time adaptation, and computational medicine.

My current work focuses on improving the robustness and adaptability of foundation models under distribution shifts, with particular interest in robot learning and vision-language-action systems. I also work on bioinformatics and multi-omics analysis for hepatocellular carcinoma.

I am interested in methods that make intelligent systems more reliable, adaptive, and deployable in real-world environments.
